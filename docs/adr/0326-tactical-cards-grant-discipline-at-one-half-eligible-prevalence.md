# ADR-0326: Tactical cards grant Discipline at one-half eligible prevalence

## Context

The initial Discipline-card decision preserved the one-through-nine shop-price
range by restricting Tactical to core cards worth at most six.

## Decision

A Tactical card grants Discipline to exactly one contained unit for three extra
gold. One half of otherwise eligible shop cards become Tactical, Tactical and
Pestiferous are mutually exclusive, and Tactical uses the flowing-water frame.

## Consequences

The rule excluded every core worth seven through nine and did not match the
owner's intended one-in-eight roll on every drawn card. ADR-0327 supersedes the
eligibility, prevalence, and resolution-order parts of this decision.
