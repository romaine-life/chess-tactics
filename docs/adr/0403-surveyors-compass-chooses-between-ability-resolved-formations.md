# ADR-0403: Surveyor's Compass chooses between ability-resolved formations

## Context

Surveyor's Compass is a Run-wide deployment lipsanon, not a unit ability. Its two layouts were
already generated by the canonical automatic placer, so Eutactic and Agminate affected both.
The player flow nevertheless exposed the Compass beside unresolved Muster Roll and Adlected
choices. Changing either upstream input regenerated the numbered layouts underneath an already
selected index.

The mounted Deployment board also projected only committed Adlected units. Even after all exact
placements were settled, the Compass offered two hidden formations and described them as
previewable. Pressing either number immediately entered Battle, so the player never saw the
alternative it purported to choose.

## Decision

- Deployment choices have one dependency order: **Muster Roll, then Adlected, then Surveyor's
  Compass**. Muster Roll first fixes which units are deploying. Adlected then fixes exact squares.
  Only those settled inputs may produce the Compass alternatives.
- Adlected owns an exact player placement. Eutactic and Agminate do not steer that unit during the
  same deployment. They continue to steer every automatically placed unit.
- Each Compass alternative is a complete seeded pass through the same automatic placer. Eutactic
  first chooses a unit's best-fit row; Agminate then chooses its station within that row. Compass
  adds no row, station, score, or override of its own.
- Changing a Muster Roll selection or an Adlected square invalidates any downstream Compass
  selection. The two alternatives are recomputed from the new settled inputs.
- Before Muster Roll and Adlected settle, the battlefield continues to show only committed
  Adlected units. The final Adlected arrival completes before the ordinary formation appears.
  When Compass is the remaining choice, its currently viewed complete friendly formation then
  appears on the same full battlefield; opponents remain unresolved.
- Compass Controls separate inspection from commitment. **View Formation 1** and **View Formation
  2** change the full-board preview without persisting the choice. **Deploy this formation** is the
  consequential Compass selection and advances directly to Battle when nothing else remains.
  It is not a second Begin Battle confirmation.
- If the two automatic layouts are identical, Compass has no meaningful choice and Deployment
  advances through the ordinary forced-choice path without showing a false comparison.

## Consequences

- Surveyor's Compass becomes a choice over the results of Eutactic and Agminate rather than a
  competing fourth placement rule.
- A formation number cannot retain meaning while its roster or exact occupied squares change.
- Comparing layouts costs explicit viewing actions, but the player can now make the choice the
  lipsanon promises instead of guessing between two labels.
- The Deployment Lab's real-player-flow launch exercises the same staged interaction and remains
  the owner-operated regression surface.
